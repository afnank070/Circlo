How CIRCLO works

CIRCLO connects people in Islamabad and Rawalpindi who own gear they don't use every day with people who need it for a weekend, a shoot, or a project. Here's what happens on both sides of a rental.

Renting an item

1

Find what you need

Browse or search by category — cameras, tools, camping gear, event equipment and more — and pick your dates on the listing page.

2

Send a request

Request the dates you want. You won't be charged yet — the owner reviews and accepts or declines first.

3

Pay the rental + deposit

Once accepted, you'll see exactly what's due — the rental fee plus a refundable security deposit — and where to send it. CIRCLO confirms receipt before the handover.

4

Pick up, use, return

Both you and the owner upload a quick "before" photo at handover and an "after" photo at return, so there's a record of the item's condition on both ends.

5

Get your deposit back

Once the owner confirms a clean return, your deposit is released. If anything's disputed, see how deposits & trust protection work.

Listing an item

1

List what you own

Add photos, a description, a category, and your price per day and deposit amount. It goes live on the marketplace right away.

2

Review requests

When someone wants to rent your item, you'll see their request under My Rentals — accept or decline it.

3

Hand over, get paid

Once CIRCLO confirms the renter's payment, hand the item over and log a "before" photo. After a clean return, you're paid out — minus CIRCLO's commission.
