Trust & deposits

Renting from a neighbour only works if both sides feel protected. Every rental on CIRCLO carries the same four layers of protection.

Verified identities

CNIC + selfie, reviewed by hand by a CIRCLO admin before anyone can list or rent.

Deposit protection

A refundable security deposit is held by CIRCLO, not the owner, and released after a clean return.

Evidence photos

Trust & Safety Fund

A reserve fund CIRCLO draws on when damage or loss goes beyond a rental's deposit.

Guide

Frequently asked questions

How does the deposit work?

When your request is accepted, you pay the rental fee plus a refundable security deposit together to CIRCLO — never directly to the owner. CIRCLO holds the deposit until you return the item, then releases it back to you once the owner confirms a clean return.

What if something gets damaged?

Either side can report a problem on the booking. An admin reviews the before/after evidence photos from both parties and decides how the deposit is applied. If the damage or loss is worth more than the deposit, CIRCLO's Trust & Safety Fund can cover the difference.

How is my ID verified?

You upload a photo of your CNIC and a selfie. A CIRCLO admin reviews every submission by hand and checks the selfie matches the CNIC photo before approving your account — it isn't automated.

Can I cancel a request?

Yes, free of charge, any time before CIRCLO confirms your payment has been received. Once payment is confirmed the rental is locked in — if your plans change after that, reach out to CIRCLO support.

Does CIRCLO take a cut?

Renters pay exactly the listed rental fee plus the deposit — no extra service fee. CIRCLO takes a 20% commission from the owner's payout once a rental completes cleanly.
