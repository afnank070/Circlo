Trust & deposits

Renting from a stranger only works if both sides feel protected. Here's exactly how CIRCLO handles identity, money, and evidence — no fine print, just how it actually works today.

Identity verification

Before renting or listing, you can verify your identity by uploading a photo of your CNIC and a selfie. An admin reviews every submission by hand — this isn't automated — so the people you're transacting with are real, verified individuals. Verified accounts carry a badge visible to the other party.

Refundable security deposit

Every rental has a deposit set by the owner, scaled to the item's value. When you rent, you pay the rental fee plus the deposit together to CIRCLO — not directly to the owner. The deposit is held until the item is returned in good condition, then it's released back to you.

Before/after photo evidence

At handover, both the renter and the owner upload a "before" photo of the item's condition. At return, both upload an "after" photo. Both sets are timestamped and kept on file — this is the record CIRCLO uses if either side raises a dispute, so neither party has to take the other's word for it.

Trust & Safety Fund

CIRCLO keeps a reserve fund set aside to cover cases where damage or loss exceeds a rental's deposit. If you report a problem, an admin reviews the evidence from both sides and decides how the deposit — and, if needed, the Trust Fund — is applied.

Reviews & ratings

After a rental completes, both sides can leave a rating and a short review. Reputation builds over time and shows on every profile and listing — a track record you can check before you book or accept.
